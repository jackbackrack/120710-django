Your browser sent the form without any data in it, so nothing was saved. That almost always means it could not read the file you attached.

The usual cause on a Mac is iCloud — the picture is in your iCloud library or iCloud Drive, and this Mac only has a placeholder for it rather than the file itself. The file chooser still shows it, so nothing looks wrong until you save.

What fixes it:

  1. Find the picture in Finder, right-click it and choose Download Now. Wait for the little cloud icon beside it to disappear.
  2. Or, if it lives in Photos, export it to your Desktop first and use that copy — dragging or exporting always makes a real file.
  3. Then come back here, choose the file again, and save.

A quick way to tell them apart: take a screenshot (⌘⇧4) and try uploading that. A screenshot is always a real file on your Mac, so if it saves and your picture does not, it is the picture that needs downloading rather than anything wrong with your account.
