{% comment %} An agenda rather than a month grid, on purpose. Shows run for weeks, so a grid fills with long bars that say little; the entries that would use grid cells are the events, and there are rarely enough of them on one page to need it. Month headings give the scanning a grid would have provided. Everything on one page: no pagination, no lazy loading, no Upcoming/Past switch. An earlier version had all three and they put a navigation decision in front of a reader who only wanted to see the programme. Upcoming first, then the past most-recent-first, so what is on now is at the top rather than buried under every show the gallery has ever hung. {% endcomment %}
